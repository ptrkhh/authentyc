  # Social Media Strategy & Content Plan for Authentyc

  ## Strategic Decision: Waitlist vs. Awareness

  ### Option A: Build Waitlist (Conversion Focus)
  **Pros:**
  - Measurable success metric (signup count)
  - Validates product-market fit before Q1 2026 launch
  - Creates urgency ("founding member rates")
  - Direct ROI - every signup is a potential customer
  - Gives you email list to nurture pre-launch

  **Cons:**
  - Smaller reach - asks for commitment immediately
  - Limits viral potential (CTAs reduce shareability)
  - May feel salesy/pushy for new accounts
  - Harder to build community early

  **Best for:** When you have product validation and need scale

  ---

  ### Option B: Build Awareness (Reach Focus)
  **Pros:**
  - Higher viral potential - entertaining/educational content spreads
  - Builds trust and credibility before the ask
  - Grows follower base organically
  - Creates "top of mind" positioning
  - More shareable, less transactional

  **Cons:**
  - Vanity metrics (likes/follows) don't equal signups
  - Harder to measure real business impact
  - May delay conversion/launch momentum
  - Could build wrong audience (entertainment seekers vs. users)

  **Best for:** When you need brand recognition first

  ---

  ### ✅ RECOMMENDED: Hybrid "Awareness → Conversion" Strategy

  **The Approach:**
  Use the **70-20-10 rule**:
  - **70% Awareness** - Problem resonance, education, entertaining insights (highly shareable)
  - **20% Demonstration** - ChatAnalyzer demo invites, product explainers (engagement)
  - **10% Conversion** - Waitlist CTAs, founding member benefits (direct ask)

  **Why This Works:**
  1. Daily awareness posts build audience and trust
  2. Demo invites let people experience value (low friction)
  3. Occasional CTAs convert warm audience (those already convinced)
  4. Viral awareness posts expand top of funnel
  5. You can adjust ratios based on what performs

  **Week 1 Example:**
  - Monday: Awareness (problem post)
  - Tuesday: Awareness (insight post)
  - Wednesday: Demo (try ChatAnalyzer)
  - Thursday: Awareness (category-specific)
  - Friday: Awareness + soft CTA
  - Saturday: Demo or case study
  - Sunday: Conversion (waitlist CTA)

  ---

  ## Content Strategy

  ### Brand Voice
  - **Tone:** Honest, direct, confident, human
  - **Style:** Short sentences. Conversational. No hype.
  - **Emotion:** Acknowledge the weight of hiring/dating/co-founder decisions
  - **Differentiation:** Privacy-first, data-driven authenticity

  ### Platform Adaptations

  **Twitter/X:**
  - Max 280 characters
  - Punchy, quotable, memeable
  - 1-2 hashtags max
  - Thread opportunities for deeper dives
  - Emoji usage: Occasional, not excessive

  **LinkedIn:**
  - Longer form (300-1000 characters)
  - Professional tone, B2B focus
  - Storytelling approach
  - Data/statistics perform well
  - Hiring category emphasis
