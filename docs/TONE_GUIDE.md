# Authentyc Brand Voice & Tone Guide

## Overview

Authentyc uses an **intentionally mixed tone** that balances approachability with credibility. This is not inconsistency—it's a strategic choice that reflects our brand positioning: simple, straightforward, and honest.

## Dual-Tone Strategy

Our voice alternates between casual and professional tones depending on the section and purpose. This mix serves two key objectives:

1. **Build emotional connection** (casual tone)
2. **Establish credibility and trust** (professional tone)

---

## Casual Tone

**Use In:**
- Hero section
- Problem section
- Call-to-action (CTA) buttons and sections
- Emotional/persuasive content

**Characteristics:**
- Use "you" and "your" (direct address)
- Contractions (don't, can't, you'll, we're)
- Short, punchy sentences
- Rhetorical questions
- Conversational rhythm
- Active voice

**Purpose:** Build approachability and emotional connection with visitors.

**Examples:**
- ✅ "Stop guessing. Start knowing."
- ✅ "See how someone really thinks before you commit."
- ✅ "Ready to stop guessing?"
- ✅ "Don't just read their resume—see how they think."

---

## Professional Tone

**Use In:**
- How It Works section
- FAQ section
- Footer
- Technical explanations
- Privacy/security information

**Characteristics:**
- Use "we" and "our" (company voice)
- Full words, no contractions (do not, cannot)
- Complete, well-structured sentences
- Factual explanations
- Formal language
- Clear, precise descriptions

**Purpose:** Establish credibility, professionalism, and trustworthiness.

**Examples:**
- ✅ "AI-powered conversation analysis reveals authentic compatibility for hiring, dating, and co-founder matching."
- ✅ "We analyze communication patterns, problem-solving style, and authentic personality."
- ✅ "You share that conversation link with us. We extract only ChatGPT's personality insights about you."
- ✅ "We only access what ChatGPT's analysis reveals, and you review it first."

---

## Section-by-Section Breakdown

| Section | Tone | Rationale |
|---------|------|-----------|
| **Hero** | Casual | First impression—needs emotional hook |
| **Problem** | Casual | Relatable pain points require empathy |
| **Solution** | Mixed | Features need clarity, benefits need appeal |
| **How It Works** | Professional | Process explanation requires precision |
| **Interactive Demo (ChatAnalyzer)** | Mixed | Instructions are clear, encouragement is warm |
| **Category Cards** | Casual | Persuasive content to drive action |
| **FAQ** | Professional | Trust-building through thorough answers |
| **Final CTA** | Casual | Last chance to convert—emotionally driven |
| **Footer** | Professional | Legal and informational content |

---

## Writing Guidelines

### DO:
- ✅ Match the tone to the section (refer to table above)
- ✅ Use contractions in casual sections
- ✅ Use direct address ("you") in persuasive content
- ✅ Use company voice ("we") in explanatory content
- ✅ Keep casual sections punchy and energetic
- ✅ Keep professional sections clear and complete

### DON'T:
- ❌ Mix tones within a single paragraph
- ❌ Use contractions in FAQ or How It Works sections
- ❌ Use overly formal language in Hero or CTAs
- ❌ Use jargon in either tone
- ❌ Be inconsistent within a single section

---

## Voice Attributes (Consistent Across Both Tones)

Regardless of tone, Authentyc's voice is always:

1. **Honest** - No hype, no exaggeration, no false promises
2. **Clear** - Avoid jargon, explain concepts simply
3. **Direct** - Get to the point quickly
4. **Confident** - State value propositions without hedging
5. **Human** - Recognize the emotional weight of matching decisions

## Examples in Context

### Hero Section (Casual)
```
Stop guessing. Start knowing.

See how someone really thinks, communicates, and solves problems before you commit.
```

### FAQ Section (Professional)
```
**Q: Is my data private?**

Yes, 100% private. Here's how it works: (1) You have a conversation with ChatGPT
where it analyzes your personality from your chat history. (2) You share that
conversation link with us. (3) We extract only ChatGPT's personality insights
about you, not your underlying chat history.
```
